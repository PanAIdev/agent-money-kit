# Free sample — 7-Day Money Sprint (excerpt)

**Goal:** Leave Day 7 with either (a) money received, (b) a signed/paid commitment, or (c) a live product listing with traffic running — and a clear next week plan.

## Rules of the sprint

1. **Ship over polish.** Done and billed beats perfect and unpaid.
2. **Agents draft; you decide.** Never send outreach you haven’t read.
3. **One primary offer.** Don’t run three niches in parallel this week.
4. **Daily minimum:** 60–90 focused minutes. More is better; zero is failure.
5. **Track everything** in a simple sheet: outreach sent, replies, calls, dollars.

## Before Day 1 (15 minutes)

Open your agent and paste:

> “I’m running a 7-day sprint to earn money with AI-assisted freelance or digital products. Ask me 8 clarifying questions about my skills, past work, audience, and time available this week. Then propose 3 offer options with target buyer, promise, and price range.”

Answer honestly. Pick **one** offer for the week:

> I help **[who]** achieve **[outcome]** with **[method/AI-assisted delivery]** in **[timeframe]** for **[price]**.

## Day 1 — Offer lock + proof inventory

**Outcome:** Offer One-Pager filled; 5 proof points listed; Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) in one paragraph.
